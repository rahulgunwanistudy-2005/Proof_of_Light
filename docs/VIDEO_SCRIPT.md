# Demo recording script

Target length is 2 minutes 45 seconds. Use your own narration and actual screen recordings. Record the PayPal and Gemini scenes after verifying those integrations with sandbox credentials. If the services are unavailable, show the local practice flow and identify its status in the narration.

| Time | Screen | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:24 | A ceramic item and an open merchant case | “A photo of a damaged item can leave a seller with questions. Proof of Light gives the buyer a guided way to show what happened and gives the merchant a case file to review.” |
| 0:24–0:56 | Open a buyer invitation. Describe damage and record an item or attach a photo. | “The buyer opens a one-use link, writes a short statement and chooses a photo or a guided capture. The screen sequence measures the light response in the uploaded frames. A matching response leaves item authenticity unverified.” |
| 0:56–1:26 | Refresh the merchant case, inspect evidence and download the PDF. Show real Gemini notes if configured. | “The merchant sees the statement, image and light diagnostics together. With buyer consent, Gemini can describe visible condition, uncertainty and a follow-up view. The merchant decides what action to take.” |
| 1:26–2:15 | Show a completed sandbox order, prepare a refund, check the amount and destination, approve, send and read PayPal’s result. | “This case is tied to a sandbox purchase. I review the exact amount and destination, approve the proposal and send it separately. The app records PayPal’s response alongside the authorization.” |
| 2:15–2:45 | Show audit history, the capture limitation and the repository. | “We tested the limits of the light signal and kept the result advisory. The audit history shows the submission, review and provider response. Proof of Light helps buyers submit clearer evidence and merchants decide with context.” |
